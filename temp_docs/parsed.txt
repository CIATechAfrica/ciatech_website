ABOUT US

Who We Are

CIATECH Africa is a youth-led social enterprise and innovation hub committed to solving complex development challenges through technology and systems-driven approaches.

We go beyond program delivery to design ecosystems, build institutions, and scale solutions that create lasting impact. Our work spans fragile and underserved regions, with a strong footprint in Northeast Nigeria and the Lake Chad region.

We collaborate with governments, development partners, academia, and the private sector to co-create solutions that are locally grounded and globally relevant.

Our Vision

An inclusive, resilient, and technology-enabled Africa where every individual has the opportunity to thrive.

Our Mission

To design and scale innovative, technology-driven solutions that unlock economic participation, strengthen communities, and transform systems.

Our Approach

We apply a systems-thinking lens to development:

Diagnose ecosystem gaps through research and data 

Design context-driven, scalable interventions 

Deploy inclusive, technology-enabled solutions 

Scale through partnerships, policy alignment, and financing 



OUR CORE PILLARS

Digital Transformation &amp; Future Skills

We build talent pipelines for the digital economy by equipping youth and women with in-demand skills in AI, data, cloud computing, and cybersecurity—while advancing indigenous language technologies to ensure no one is left behind.

Inclusive Economies &amp; Entrepreneurship

We unlock economic participation by supporting startups and SMEs with access to skills, markets, finance, and cross-border trade opportunities—particularly within the AfCFTA framework and the Lake Chad region.

Protection, Gender &amp; Social Inclusion

We design inclusive systems that empower women, youth, and persons with disabilities to fully participate in economic and social life, with a strong focus on dignity, safety, and equity.

Peacebuilding &amp; Community Resilience

We leverage technology and youth-led innovation to strengthen social cohesion, promote civic engagement, and address the root causes of conflict in fragile settings.

Governance, Policy &amp; Institutional Strengthening

We support institutions with digital tools, policy insights, and capacity development to improve governance, transparency, and service delivery.

Climate Resilience &amp; Green Innovation

We accelerate climate-smart solutions—from sustainable agriculture to waste-to-energy innovations—enabling communities to adapt, mitigate, and thrive in the face of climate change.

FLAGSHIP INITIATIVES

Kanuri AI

A pioneering indigenous language AI platform expanding digital access for millions of underserved speakers—unlocking inclusion in education, governance, and digital services.

Youth Innovation &amp; Civic Tech Program

Empowering young innovators to build solutions that enhance transparency, civic participation, and governance.

Women in Business Program

Empowering women entrepreneurs across Northeast Nigeria and the Lake Chad region through:

Access to skills, mentorship, and market opportunities 

Support in business planning, financing, and scaling 

Networks to increase visibility and influence in local and regional markets 

This initiative transforms women-led enterprises into sustainable engines of economic growth, aligning with CIATECH’s mission of inclusive economic participation.

STEM Champ Initiative

Developing the next generation of African innovators by:

Nurturing youth talent in Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics (STEM) 

Providing hands-on training, mentorship, and access to innovation labs 

Linking participants to internships, research projects, and startup opportunities 

STEM Champ is designed to close the skills gap, promote gender equity in STEM, and create a talent pipeline for Africa’s innovation ecosystem.
